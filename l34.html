And There Shall Be No More Curse

Stanza 22 of 23 · Lesson 34: Eschatology 2 — God’s Plan through Israel and the Church

Download or open the MP3

Long before, God had brought Abram out under the stars

and passed between the pieces Himself, a burning lamp in the dark:

In thee shall all families of the earth be blessed.

Through Israel came the covenants, the oracles, and Christ according to the flesh.

He came unto His own, and His own received Him not;

so for a while the nation stands aside,

and the casting away of them is the reconciling of the world:

blindness in part, until the fulness of the Gentiles be come in.

Before the mountains were brought forth, He is God.

He needs nothing. He gives all things.

And there shall be no more curse.
