And There Shall Be No More Curse

Stanza 3 of 23 · Lesson 15: Theology 2 — God’s Rule over All

Download or open the MP3

His counsel stands. He does whatever He pleases;

none can stay His hand, or say unto Him, What doest thou?

He removes kings and sets up kings;

Nebuchadnezzar ate grass like an ox until he knew it.

Joseph’s brothers sold him for silver and meant it for evil;

God meant it unto good.

And one day wicked hands would take the Lamb

by the determinate counsel of God—

the sinners guilty, the purpose holy, the hand unstayed.

Before the mountains were brought forth, He is God.

He needs nothing. He gives all things.

And there shall be no more curse.
