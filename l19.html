And There Shall Be No More Curse

Stanza 7 of 23 · Lesson 19: Christology 1 — Who He Is

Download or open the MP3

The way through is not a road but a Person,

whose goings forth have been from of old, from everlasting.

In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God;

all things were made by Him.

He said, Before Abraham was, I am, and they took up stones.

Promised in Eden, foretold by the prophets, He is the Christ;

and Thomas, seeing Him, said, My Lord and my God—

and He did not correct him.

Before the mountains were brought forth, He is God.

He needs nothing. He gives all things.

And there shall be no more curse.
