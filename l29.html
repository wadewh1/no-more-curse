And There Shall Be No More Curse

Stanza 17 of 23 · Lesson 29: Angelology 1 — The Enemy: Who He Is and Why He Fell

Download or open the MP3

But there is an enemy in this story, and he is not God’s equal.

He was made, a creature, and he was lifted up with pride

and said in his heart, I will be like the most High.

He is a liar, and the father of it;

he accuses the brethren before our God day and night.

He could not touch Job past the line God drew.

The holy angels stood, and still minister to the heirs of salvation;

and when John fell at an angel’s feet, the angel said,

See thou do it not: worship God.

Before the mountains were brought forth, He is God.

He needs nothing. He gives all things.

And there shall be no more curse.
