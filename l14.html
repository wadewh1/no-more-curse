And There Shall Be No More Curse

Stanza 2 of 23 · Lesson 14: Theology 1 — Who God Is in Himself

Download or open the MP3

This God does not change.

Saul turned aside, and the kingdom was torn from him

as the hem of Samuel’s mantle tore,

but the Strength of Israel did not lie, nor turn as a man turns.

He fainteth not, neither is weary;

no chisel can carve Him, for God is a Spirit.

His ways are past finding out—

and still, the only begotten Son has declared Him.

Before the mountains were brought forth, He is God.

He needs nothing. He gives all things.

And there shall be no more curse.
