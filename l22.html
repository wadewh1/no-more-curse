And There Shall Be No More Curse

Stanza 10 of 23 · Lesson 22: Christology 4 — The King

Download or open the MP3

A cloud received Him out of their sight,

and two men in white apparel said, This same Jesus shall so come.

Now He sits on the right hand of the Majesty on high,

with a name which is above every name,

ever living to make intercession,

building His church, preparing a place,

until His enemies be made His footstool.

Before the mountains were brought forth, He is God.

He needs nothing. He gives all things.

And there shall be no more curse.
